## Step 2: Scoping Call

### VBD Execution Playbook

Call scheduling: CSAM schedules and hosts a 30-60 minute scoping call 1-2 weeks in advance of the delivery with the customer and CSA.

### Using M365 Copilot to support VBD Delivery

When using teams meetings for VBD delivery phases (pre-scoping, scoping, delivery, close-out), ask your customer for permission to enable transcription in support of meeting notes and summarization. With this permission, and with transcription enabled, you can leverage Copilot meeting summaries to capture anything missed, as well as using the output to make drafting varioues VBD deliverables and artifacts more efficient.

### Key Objectives

Ensure scope, objectives, format, prerequisites, and deliverables are understood by the customer.
Confirm engagement specifics.
Confirm dates, logistics and attendees.

### Make sure the delivery is a good fit: Start with the outcome

### After the meeting, complete the scoping phase

- Following your scoping discussion, complete the "Kick-Off" template on the IPKit. This contains a template for the agenda of the engagement. Once this has been filled in, you can e-mail the customer to confirm the agenda.

- Consider sharing a screenshot of the finished agenda slide for the customer to confirm. If modifying any standard agenda items, be sure to call out the change so that it's easy for the customer to see and sign off.

- Send an email summary of the activity scope and schedule agreed upon, and request approval in writing for this work to proceed.


### Technical Requirements and Pre-Requisites

#### General Objectives

- Reviewing and optimizing conditional access policies.
- Discuss potential optimizations.
- Pilot implementation of the scoped recommendations.

#### Out of Scope

- MS Intune implementation for managing devices.
- Configuring device identity (Entra Hybrid Join).
- Troubleshooting third party IDPs such as Ping Federate, Okta.
- Take ownership of the project, manage project team or individuals, produce Project status reports, manage deadlines and project status, documentation creation.

## Step 3: Delivery

The customer delivery is comprised of two phases:

- Kickoff: Short kickoff meeting to reiterate the delivery objectives, timeline, commitment, and agreed upon closeout meeting time.
- Technical VBD Delivery: This is the bulk of the delivery and value for the customer.
